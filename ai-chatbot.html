PROJECT 04

AI Chatbot

A voice-based AI chatbot developed using an ESP32 microcontroller and the Xiaozhi AI platform, enabling interactive communication through voice input and audio output.

Overview

The project involved developing a compact AI chatbot using an ESP32-based hardware platform. The system was designed to provide interactive voice communication with an AI assistant through connected audio components.

Procedure

The system captures the user's voice through an I²S microphone and processes the interaction through the Xiaozhi AI platform. The AI-generated response is then delivered through a connected speaker, while an OLED display provides visual feedback during operation.

FOCUS

AI-based voice interaction

SYSTEM

ESP32-based chatbot

INPUT

I²S microphone

OUTPUT

Speaker and OLED display
