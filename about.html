ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Practical by approach.

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student driven by curiosity about how microcontrollers, processors, and electronic systems work at their core. My interest in understanding their working principles led me to choose ECE, a field that offers a broad range of opportunities and applications.

My primary areas of interest are automation and embedded systems, where hardware and software come together to create practical solutions. I enjoy approaching problems by first understanding the underlying theory and then applying that knowledge through hands-on experimentation and implementation.

Through my engineering projects, I have gained practical experience in circuit building and analysis, soldering, 3D printing, and prototype development. Working on systems involving automation and robotics has helped me understand the importance of integrating electronic, mechanical, and control aspects when developing a working solution.

I am currently focused on strengthening my knowledge of embedded systems, electronics, and robotics while continuing to develop my technical and problem-solving abilities. I particularly enjoy the process of taking an idea, breaking down the problem, brainstorming possible approaches, and gradually turning the most suitable idea into a working prototype.

My goal is to become an engineer who remains curious, adaptable, and willing to explore new technologies, while approaching challenges with creativity, structured thinking, and a problem-solving mindset. I believe that continuous experimentation and learning are essential to becoming a better engineer.
