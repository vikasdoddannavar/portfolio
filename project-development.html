PROJECT 04

Project Development & Team Collaboration

This project involved a hands-on engineering development session carried out in an Idea Lab. Our team worked together on hardware assembly, circuit connections, wiring, testing, and debugging. The experience helped us translate theoretical concepts into practical applications. Team collaboration improved our problem-solving, communication, and technical skills. The session provided valuable practical exposure to engineering tools, equipment, and systematic troubleshooting.
