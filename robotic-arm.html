Automated Material Handling Robotic Arm

Overview

An automated robotic arm designed to pick, move, and place materials between designated locations, demonstrating robotic control, motor actuation, sensing, and automation.

Procedure

The robotic arm is controlled to perform a sequence of material-handling movements. The system coordinates the arm's joints and actuators so that it can reach a designated position, pick up a material, move it to another location, and release it. The process demonstrates coordinated motion, control, and automated handling.
